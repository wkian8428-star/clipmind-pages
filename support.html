ClipMind Support

🚀 Quick Start

1
Open ClipMind and complete the onboarding setup
2
Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, select ClipMind and enable "Allow Full Access"
3
In any app, long-press the globe icon to switch to ClipMind keyboard and access your clipboard history

❓ FAQ

Why isn't clipboard content saved automatically?

iOS restricts apps from reading the clipboard in the background. Use these methods to save content:

  • Content is automatically captured when you open ClipMind
  • Use the Share Extension: tap "Share" in any app → select ClipMind
  • Use the ClipMind keyboard extension to paste from history

How do I enable Face ID protection?

Go to ClipMind → Settings → Privacy & Security → enable Face ID. Password-type content is automatically encrypted.

Is iCloud sync safe?

Yes. iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit with encrypted transmission. WingQing cannot access your data. Encrypted sensitive content (like passwords) does not participate in sync.

How do I delete all data?

Go to ClipMind → Settings → Data Management → Clear Trash. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

What about the share extension?

In any app, select text or content → tap the Share button → choose ClipMind from the share sheet. The content will be saved to your clipboard history.
